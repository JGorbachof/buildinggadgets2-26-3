## Mechanical release-candidate validation (2026-11-25)

Adapted the sparse copy packing repair from voidsip, PR #261, commit 47bded5d0c75e53469100b195ba02d409c18c966 (https://github.com/Direwolf20-MC/BuildingGadgets2/pull/261). Preserves the four-field packed format and bounds/palette validation. Unlike the proposed patch, uses VOID_AIR for absent cells to avoid destructive replacement of unrecorded positions. Explicit air stays air. Network imports are limited to 100,000 cells before allocation, malformed bounds or indices rejected before changing templates, and ordinary imports cannot create cut cargo. Fixed end-of-list mode rotation and missing copy/undo data guards.

Cut clipboard completion now retains unplaced cells, marks completed coordinates as skip cells, and updates live gadget cache/mode. Prevents starting a new cut while cargo remains. The internal cut-progress tracker is not normal undo history. Redprint player transfers move cargo instead of duplicating it; stale consumed items cannot load twice, and named admin-only recovery archives remain available. This deliberately changes normal redprint transfer semantics, not the administrator recovery command. Recheck basic world interaction on processing a queue. Cut admission checks energy as upstream; placement debits energy as upstream. No claim that every cancellation, forced process crash, concurrent world edit, protection mod, or insufficient-material race is atomic. Client and actual multiplayer still require modpack QC.
